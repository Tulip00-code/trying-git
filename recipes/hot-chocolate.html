Cozy Hot Choco

    Ingredients

  1. Milk: 2 cups full-cream milk

  2. Cocoa Powder: 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder

  3. Real Chocolate: ⅓ cup (around 50g) milk or dark chocolate, finely chopped

  4. Sugar: 1 to 2 tablespoons sugar (adjust to taste)

  5. Flavor Boosts: A splash of vanilla extract & a tiny pinch of salt

    6. Step By Step Guide

  6. 1. Heat & Whisk Base • Pour the milk into a small saucepan over medium-low heat. • Add the cocoa powder, sugar, and pinch of salt. Whisk continuously until the milk gets warm and the powder completely dissolves without lumps.

    2. Melt the Chocolate • Turn the heat down to low. Drop in your finely chopped real chocolate. • Stir constantly for 2 minutes until the chocolate melts entirely into the milk, making it smooth, thick, and creamy. Do not let the mixture boil.

    3. Finish & Serve • Remove the saucepan from the stove. Stir in your vanilla extract. • Pour into mugs and immediately top with marshmallows, whipped cream, or a dusting of extra cocoa powder.
