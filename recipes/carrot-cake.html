Moist Homemade Carrot Cake

Description

This is an incredibly moist, perfectly spiced carrot cake packed with freshly grated carrots, warm cinnamon, and a sweet, velvety cream cheese frosting layer.

Ingredients

    =>For Sponge

  • Flour: 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour (maida)
  • Baking Agents: 2 tsp baking powder & 1 tsp baking soda
  • Spices & Salt: 2 tsp ground cinnamon, ½ tsp ground nutmeg, & ½ tsp fine salt
  • Sugars: 1 cup (200g) packed brown sugar & ½ cup (100g) white granulated sugar
  • Oil: 1 cup (240ml) neutral vegetable oil or corn oil
  • Eggs: 4 large eggs (at room temperature)
  • Carrots: 3 cups (approx. 300g) finely grated fresh carrots (peeled before grating)
  • Extract: 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • Crunch (Optional): 1 cup finely chopped walnuts or pecans

    • =>For Cream Cheese Frosting

  • Cream Cheese: 16 oz (450g) brick-style cream cheese (softened to room temperature)
  • Butter: ½ cup (115g) unsalted butter (softened to room temperature)
  • Sugar: 4 cups (480g) powdered/icing sugar (sifted to eliminate lumps)
  • Extract & Salt: 1½ tsp pure vanilla extract & a pinch of salt

Step By Step Guide

  1. Prep • Heat & Line: Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease two 9-inch cake pans and line the bottoms with parchment paper.

  2. Batter • Mix Wets: In a large bowl, whisk the oil, brown sugar, white sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract until completely smooth. • Add Drys: Stir in the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Mix gently just until the flour disappears. • Fold Carrots: Toss in the grated carrots (and walnuts if you want them) and stir to combine.

  3. Bake • Oven Time: Divide the batter between your pans. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let them cool completely.

  4. Frosting • Whip It: Beat the room-temperature cream cheese and butter together until smooth. Gradually beat in the powdered sugar, a pinch of salt, and vanilla until fluffy.

  5. Assemble • Stack & Frost: Place one cake layer down, spread a layer of frosting on top, place the second cake layer on top, and use the rest of the frosting to cover the top and sides. Refrigerate for 30 minutes before slicing.
