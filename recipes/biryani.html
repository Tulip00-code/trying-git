Chicken Biryani

Description

This is a traditional way of making Biryani

Ingredients

    => Chicken Marinade

  • Chicken: 1 kg (preferably bone-in, cut into medium pieces)
  • Yogurt: 1 cup (whisked plain curd)
  • Red Chili Powder: 1.5 to 2 teaspoons
  • Coriander & Cumin Powder: 1 teaspoon each
  • Lemon Juice: 2 tablespoons
  • Salt: To taste (approx. 1 teaspoon)

    • => For The Rice

  • Basmati Rice: 3 cups (aged, long-grain; soaked for 30 minutes)
  • Salt: 2 tablespoons (the boiling water should taste like sea water)
  • Vinegar or Lemon Juice: 1 teaspoon (keeps the rice grains separate and white)

    • =>Masala Base

  • Oil / Ghee: ½ cup
  • Tomatoes: 2 medium (chopped or pureed)
  • Potatoes: 2 to 3 large (optional; peeled and halved)
  • Green Chilies: 4 to 5 (slit lengthwise)
  • Onions: 4 large (thinly sliced; fried until crispy golden brown)

    • =>Dum

  • 1 handful each (finely chopped)
  • Saffron Milk: A pinch of saffron strands dissolved in ¼ cup of warm milk
  • Kewra Water or Rose Water: 1 teaspoon (for that signature restaurant aroma)

Step By Step Guide

1. Marinate Mix chicken, yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and all marinade spices in a bowl. Rest for 30 minutes.

2. Fry Onions Heat ghee/oil in a large, heavy pot. Fry onions until golden and crispy. Take out 2/3 of the onions for later; leave 1/3 in the pot.

3. Cook Chicken Gravy Add tomatoes and green chilies to the pot with the remaining onions. Cook until soft. Add the marinated chicken and simmer for 15–20 minutes until cooked through and the gravy is thick.

4. Par-Boil Rice In a separate pot, boil water with whole spices, salt, and lemon juice. Add soaked rice and cook for 5–7 minutes until it is 70% cooked (still has a bite). Drain completely.

5. Layer & Dum • Layer: Leave the chicken gravy at the bottom of the pot. Layer the drained rice evenly on top. • Garnish: Scatter the saved fried onions, mint, and cilantro over the rice. Drizzle with saffron milk and kewra water. • Steam (Dum): Seal the lid tightly with foil or dough. Cook on low heat for 20 minutes (use a flat skillet/tawa underneath to prevent burning).

6. Serve Turn off the heat and let it rest sealed for 10 minutes. Gently scoop from the sides to plate without breaking the rice grains.
